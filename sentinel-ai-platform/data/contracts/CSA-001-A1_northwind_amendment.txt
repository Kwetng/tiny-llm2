AMENDMENT AGREEMENT No. 1
Reference: CSA-001-A1
Amending: CSA-001
between SENTINEL BANK INTERNATIONAL PLC ("Party A") and NORTHWIND ENERGY PLC ("Party B")

The parties agree that, with effect from 1 July 2025, the Credit Support Annex referenced CSA-001 is amended as follows:

1. The Threshold with respect to Party B is amended to GBP 1,000,000.
2. The Minimum Transfer Amount with respect to each party is amended to GBP 100,000.

All other terms of the Credit Support Annex remain unchanged.

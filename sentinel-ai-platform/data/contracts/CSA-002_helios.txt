CREDIT SUPPORT ANNEX
to the Schedule to the ISDA 2002 Master Agreement
Reference: CSA-002
dated as of 4 October 2021
between SENTINEL BANK INTERNATIONAL PLC ("Party A") and HELIOS ASSET MANAGEMENT LLP ("Party B")

Paragraph 11. Elections and Variables

(a) "Base Currency" means EUR.

(b) Credit Support Obligations.
(i) "Threshold" means, with respect to Party A: zero; with respect to Party B: zero.
(ii) "Minimum Transfer Amount" means, with respect to each party, EUR 500,000.
(iii) "Independent Amount" means, with respect to Party B, 2% of the aggregate notional of all Transactions.
(iv) Eligible Credit Support:
  (A) cash in EUR: 100%
  (B) German Bunds with remaining maturity of not more than 10 years: 97%

(c) "Valuation Agent" means Party A.
(d) "Notification Time" means 12:00 noon, London time, on a Local Business Day.
(e) Governing Law. This Annex is governed by English law.

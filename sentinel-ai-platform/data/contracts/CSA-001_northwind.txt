CREDIT SUPPORT ANNEX
to the Schedule to the ISDA 2002 Master Agreement
Reference: CSA-001
dated as of 12 March 2019
between SENTINEL BANK INTERNATIONAL PLC ("Party A") and NORTHWIND ENERGY PLC ("Party B")

Paragraph 11. Elections and Variables

(a) Base Currency and Eligible Currency.
(i) "Base Currency" means GBP.

(b) Credit Support Obligations.
(i) "Threshold" means, with respect to Party A: GBP 5,000,000; with respect to Party B: GBP 2,500,000.
(ii) "Minimum Transfer Amount" means, with respect to each party, GBP 250,000.
(iii) "Independent Amount" means, with respect to each party, zero.
(iv) Eligible Credit Support. The following items qualify as Eligible Credit Support, with the Valuation Percentage shown:
  (A) cash in GBP: 100%
  (B) UK Gilts with remaining maturity of not more than 5 years: 98%
  (C) UK Gilts with remaining maturity of more than 5 years: 95%

(c) Valuation and Timing.
(i) "Valuation Agent" means Party A.
(ii) "Notification Time" means 1:00 p.m., London time, on a Local Business Day.

(d) Governing Law. This Annex is governed by English law.

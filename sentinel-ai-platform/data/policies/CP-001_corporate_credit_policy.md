## 1 Scope
### 1.1 Application
This policy applies to all credit exposures to corporate clients booked by EMEA entities, including term loans, revolving credit facilities, guarantees and derivative counterparty lines.

## 2 Credit approval authorities
### 2.1 Delegated authority
Relationship managers may not approve credit. Credit officers hold personal delegated authority up to GBP 25 million per obligor group for investment-grade clients and up to GBP 10 million for sub-investment-grade clients.
### 2.2 Credit committee
Exposures above personal delegated authority, and all exposures to clients rated 7 or worse on the internal rating scale, must be approved by the EMEA Credit Committee, which meets weekly.
### 2.3 Tokyo referral
Any single obligor group exposure above GBP 250 million must also be referred to the Group Credit Committee in Tokyo before approval.

## 3 Financial covenants and leverage
### 3.1 Standard covenants
Term loans to sub-investment-grade borrowers must include a leverage covenant and an interest cover covenant, tested quarterly on a rolling twelve-month basis.
### 3.2 Leverage appetite
The bank's appetite for senior leverage is up to 4.0x Debt to EBITDA for sub-investment-grade corporates. Transactions above 4.0x are classified as leveraged lending and must follow the Leveraged Lending Guidelines. Transactions above 6.0x are outside appetite and require a documented exception approved by the Chief Risk Officer.
### 3.3 Interest cover
Interest cover, EBITDA divided by net interest expense, must not fall below 3.0x at origination for sub-investment-grade borrowers.

## 4 Annual review and watchlist
### 4.1 Annual review
Every corporate credit limit must be reviewed at least annually. A limit that is not reviewed within 30 days of its review date is suspended for new drawings.
### 4.2 Watchlist triggers
A client must be added to the watchlist within 5 business days if a covenant is breached, a payment is more than 30 days past due, or the internal rating is downgraded by two notches or more.

## 5 Covenant breaches
### 5.1 Escalation
A covenant breach must be reported by the relationship manager to Credit Risk within 2 business days of becoming aware of it. Waivers of financial covenants require approval at the same authority level as the original credit.

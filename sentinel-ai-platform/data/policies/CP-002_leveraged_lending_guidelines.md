## 1 Definition
### 1.1 Leveraged transaction
A leveraged transaction is any loan to a sub-investment-grade borrower where total Debt to EBITDA exceeds 4.0x at origination, or any loan to a borrower owned by a financial sponsor.

## 2 Underwriting standards
### 2.1 Repayment capacity
The borrower must be able to repay at least 50 percent of total debt within seven years from free cash flow in the base case.
### 2.2 EBITDA adjustments
Add-backs to EBITDA for projected synergies or cost savings are capped at 20 percent of reported EBITDA and must be supported by third-party diligence.
### 2.3 Hold limits
The final hold for any single leveraged transaction must not exceed GBP 75 million, and underwriting commitments above GBP 150 million need a syndication plan approved by the Leveraged Finance Committee.

## 3 Monitoring
### 3.1 Quarterly reporting
Leveraged transactions must be reported to the Leveraged Finance Committee every quarter, including compliance with covenants and progress on syndication.

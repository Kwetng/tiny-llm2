## 1 Margin calls
### 1.1 Daily calls
Variation margin calls are calculated daily for every counterparty with a Credit Support Annex and must be issued by 11:00 London time.
### 1.2 Minimum transfer amount
No call is made when the amount due is below the minimum transfer amount agreed in the Credit Support Annex.

## 2 Disputes
### 2.1 Dispute resolution
A margin dispute above GBP 1 million, or any dispute lasting more than 3 business days, must be escalated to the Head of Collateral Operations and reported to Credit Risk.
### 2.2 Portfolio reconciliation
Portfolios with more than 500 trades with one counterparty must be reconciled daily; smaller portfolios weekly.

## 3 Eligible collateral
### 3.1 Standard eligibility
Unless the Credit Support Annex says otherwise, eligible collateral is cash in GBP, USD or EUR with a 0 percent haircut, and UK, US or German government bonds with haircuts between 0.5 and 4 percent by maturity.

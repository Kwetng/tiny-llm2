## 1 Inside information
### 1.1 Definition
Material non-public information, MNPI, is precise information not generally available which, if made public, would be likely to have a significant effect on the price of a financial instrument.
### 1.2 Information barriers
Staff on the private side, such as deal teams working on acquisitions or financings, must not share MNPI with public-side staff, including sales, trading and research. Wall-crossing a public-side employee requires prior approval from Compliance, which records the crossing on the insider list.

## 2 Research
### 2.1 Publication
Investment research must be distributed to all clients entitled to receive it at the same time. Traders must not trade on the contents of research before it has been published.
### 2.2 Use of AI tools
Research analysts may use approved AI tools to summarise public information. They must not paste MNPI or client-confidential information into any AI tool that is not approved for restricted data.

## 1 Limit framework
### 1.1 Value at Risk
The EMEA Rates desk has a one-day 99 percent Value at Risk limit of GBP 4.5 million. Utilisation above 90 percent must be reported to Market Risk the same day.
### 1.2 Stressed loss limit
The desk's loss under the bank's severe stress scenario must not exceed GBP 60 million.
### 1.3 PV01 limits
Aggregate interest rate sensitivity is limited to GBP 350,000 per basis point across all currencies, and GBP 150,000 per basis point in any single currency curve.

## 2 Breaches
### 2.1 Limit breach procedure
A hard limit breach must be escalated to the Head of Market Risk within one hour and the position reduced within the limit by the end of the next business day unless a temporary increase is approved.
### 2.2 Temporary increases
Temporary limit increases may be granted by the Head of Market Risk for up to 10 business days and must be reported to the Asset and Liability Committee.

## 1 Transaction
### 1.1 Purpose
Project Heron is the proposed acquisition of Kestrel Logistics plc by Aurora Industrial Holdings, not yet announced to the market.
### 1.2 Facilities
The bank will underwrite a GBP 420 million senior term loan B and a GBP 60 million revolving credit facility, with a final hold target of GBP 70 million after syndication.

## 2 Pricing and leverage
### 2.1 Pricing
The term loan B margin is SONIA plus 4.25 percent with a 1 percent original issue discount.
### 2.2 Leverage
Opening senior leverage is 5.2x Debt to EBITDA, which is a leveraged transaction under the Leveraged Lending Guidelines.

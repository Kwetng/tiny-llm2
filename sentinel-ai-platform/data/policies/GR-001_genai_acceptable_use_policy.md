## 1 Approved tools
### 1.1 Approved channels
Staff may only use generative AI through the bank's approved AI gateway or tools listed in the AI inventory. Public consumer AI services must not be used for any bank information.
### 1.2 Data classes
Public and internal information may be used with any approved tool. Confidential information may only be used with tools approved for confidential data. Restricted information, including MNPI and deal-team material, may only be used with tools approved for restricted data and only by staff entitled to see it.

## 2 Human responsibility
### 2.1 Accountability
The member of staff who uses an AI output remains accountable for it. AI output must be checked before it is sent to a client, used in a credit decision or relied on for a regulatory submission.
### 2.2 Automated decisions
No decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on an individual may be made solely by an AI system.

## 3 Incidents
### 3.1 Reporting
Suspected AI incidents, including data leakage, harmful output or prompt injection, must be reported to the AI Engineering on-call team through the service desk within 24 hours.
